ABOUT US

Welcome to Academy of Excellence

Education That Creates a Better Future

Academy of Excellence was established on 9th November 1990 to provide quality education in Karachi. Our goal is to help students improve their knowledge, skills and confidence through experienced teachers and modern teaching methods.

We offer different educational programs including Excellence Model School, Excellence Intermediate College, Excellence Coaching Center, Excellence Institute of Information Technology, Excellence Aptitude Test Program, Anglo Vision English Learning Institute, and Excellence Publication House.

Our students have achieved good positions and results in different examinations. We focus on academic learning, English communication, technology, test preparation and career guidance. We also provide modern learning facilities such as computer and science laboratories, multimedia and audio-visual learning. Our aim is to prepare students for higher education, professional careers and a successful future.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, modern and meaningful education that helps students achieve their academic and professional goals.
